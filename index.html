Hard Close is a controller practice for companies with a raise, an audit or a sale ahead of them. I run the close, the cash and the balance sheet, so that the numbers you hand over are the numbers that survive the questions.

hard close

noun

The books closed, reconciled and tied to source on a fixed day every month, to a standard that an investor, an auditor or a buyer could inspect without notice.

OriginA rule I worked under for years inside large finance teams, where the close had a day and the day did not move.

01 The standard

Three things the books should do, every month.

Most companies buy accounting at the lowest price available and find out what that bought them in the week it matters. These are the three things I build so that week is uneventful.

The close

Finish on a calendar

The close has a date, a checklist and an owner, and it finishes on the same day each month whether or not anyone is chasing it. I have taken a fifteen-day close down to five by writing the work down as it was done and putting it on a schedule.

The cash

Say where the money is

A cash file built from a vendor budget and checked against the bank every day, so the number you plan around in the morning is the number that is actually there. It gets built first, because it is the one report that changes a decision you are making today.

The balance sheet

Stand up to inspection

Every account reconciled and tied to a source document, so that diligence, an audit or a lender's review becomes a matter of opening the file. Clean books shorten that process, and the weeks saved are weeks spent running the company.

03 Engagement

How it starts, and how it runs.

01

Ninety minutes

You send read access to the books and give me ninety minutes. I tell you what I see, whether or not you hire me, and if there is nothing worth telling you I will say that.

02

The cleanup

Whatever the review turns up gets fixed as a finite project with a defined scope: reconciliations brought current, unrecorded activity booked, the balance sheet tied out, and the procedures written down while the work is being done.

03

The close

From there I run the function as your controller on a flat monthly retainer, which covers the close, the cash file, the reporting package, and the audit or diligence when it comes. It is built so that the day you no longer need someone at my level, you can hand it over without anything breaking.

04 About
