01   What I do

Six things, every month.

This is the work I take on as your controller, and it runs on the same schedule every month. Open a line to see what it means in practice.

RefThe workWhat you get
The books close on day five, every month, on a written schedule. Every balance sheet account is reconciled and tied to a source document before the month is called done.
Invoices go out on time, what is outstanding gets followed up, and the receivables balance ties to the ledger, so you can see the cash that is coming and when.
Every bill is read line by line before it is approved, paid on a schedule and recorded in the right period. This is where duplicate subscriptions and wrong charges get caught.
A cash file built from a vendor budget and checked against the bank every day, so the number you plan around is the number that is actually there.
A monthly package you can send to your board, your investors or your lender as it is, with the lines that moved explained before anyone has to ask.
The books have an owner. When an investor, an auditor or a lender has a question about a number, it comes to me and it gets answered from the file.
Run by one senior person

I am not only closing the books. I am working the question with you.

02   Financial quarterback

The useful part of finance is having somebody who knows your numbers well enough to sit with you when a decision has a financial side. A hire, a price change, a term sheet, a lender asking for something: I work out with you what it does to the cash and the books before you commit.

A quarterback calls the play and makes sure it gets run. He does not play every position. When something belongs to your tax preparer, your auditor or your lawyer, I bring them in, hand them clean numbers and stay on it until it is answered. What I own is the books and the follow-through.

03   Engagement

How it starts, and how it runs.

There is no deck and no discovery call before the discovery call. It starts with me reading your books.

01

Ninety minutes

You send read access and give me ninety minutes. I tell you what I see, whether or not you hire me, and if there is nothing worth telling you I will say that.

02

The cleanup

Whatever turns up gets fixed as a finite project with a defined scope: reconciliations brought current, unrecorded activity booked, the balance sheet tied out, and the procedures written down while the work is being done.

03

The monthly rhythm

From there I run the function as your controller on a flat monthly retainer: the five-day close, receivables, payables, the cash file and the reporting package, plus the audit or diligence when it comes.
